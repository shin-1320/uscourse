When there is no English menu

Photo menus are common and usually enough. If the menu is text only, pointing at what someone else is eating is normal and nobody minds. Papago handles Korean food names better than Google Translate.

Do you have an English menu? — 영어 메뉴 있어요? (yeong-eo me-nyu it-eo-yo)

What do you recommend? — 뭐가 맛있어요? (mwo-ga ma-sit-eo-yo)

Common questions

Do restaurants in Busan have English menus?

Some do, mostly in Haeundae, Nampo and Seomyeon. Smaller neighbourhood restaurants usually have Korean-only menus, though photo menus are common.

How do I order without an English menu?

Point at a photo or at what someone nearby is eating. This is normal and staff are used to it. Papago translates Korean food names more accurately than Google Translate.
