How to eat Sundae-gukbap

What locals do without thinking, written down.

Sundae-gukbap arrives with a step that changes how it tastes, and most visitors miss it because nobody thinks to mention it. Get the order wrong and the dish is fine but flat.

How locals eat it

  1. Taste the broth first — Same pork broth, served with sundae and offal.
  2. Season with saeujeot — Salted shrimp is the local way. Perilla powder if offered.
  3. Dip sundae in salt — Some shops give salt or ssamjang for the sausage slices.

Good to know

Sundae (순대) is a sausage filled with sweet potato noodles and pig's blood. Despite how that sounds, it tastes mild and slightly sweet — the blood works as a binder, much like black pudding in Britain or morcilla in Spain. The texture is soft and chewy. The bowl also comes with naejang (내장, pork offal) by default. If that is not for you: · "순대만 주세요" = sundae only · "고기만 주세요" = plain pork only Dadaegi (red chili paste) is often mixed in too — say "다대기 빼주세요" for a mild bowl.

Say this when you order — not after:

순대만 주세요
