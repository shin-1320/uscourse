How to eat Gomjangeo (grilled hagfish)

What locals do without thinking, written down.

Gomjangeo (grilled hagfish) arrives with a step that changes how it tastes, and most visitors miss it because nobody thinks to mention it. Get the order wrong and the dish is fine but flat.

How locals eat it

  1. Let it cook through — Grilled at your table. Wait until fully done.
  2. Wrap it up — Perilla leaf or lettuce + gomjangeo + garlic.
  3. Finish with fried rice — Order bokkeumbap at the end — cooked in the same pan.

Good to know

Hagfish sounds alarming, but it eats much like eel — firm, springy, smoky. Busan is where Koreans come to eat it, so this is the place to try it. Fair warning: at some stalls it is grilled very fresh, and the pieces move on the hot plate for a moment. Locals find this normal; first-timers usually laugh, then eat it anyway. · Mild option → "소금구이 주세요" (salt-grilled) · Don't leave without → "볶음밥 주세요" — they fry rice in the leftover sauce right on your grill. This is the part locals come back for.

Say this when you order — not after:

소금구이 주세요

How do I order Gomjangeo (grilled hagfish) less spicy?

Say "소금구이 주세요" when you order. Once the chilli is mixed in it cannot be removed.
