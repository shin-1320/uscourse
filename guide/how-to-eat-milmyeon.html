How to eat Milmyeon

What locals do without thinking, written down.

Milmyeon arrives with a step that changes how it tastes, and most visitors miss it because nobody thinks to mention it. Get the order wrong and the dish is fine but flat.

How locals eat it

  1. Cut the noodles once — Scissors are on the table. One cross-cut is enough.
  2. Add vinegar & mustard — One splash each. Mustard is sharp — go easy.
  3. Mix well — Combine everything before the first bite.

Good to know

There are two kinds and they are completely different: · Mul milmyeon (물밀면) — cold broth, mild, refreshing. The safe choice. · Bibim milmyeon (비빔밀면) — no broth, mixed with red chili sauce. Spicy. Milmyeon was created by North Korean refugees in Busan after the Korean War, using wheat because buckwheat was scarce — which is why you find it here and almost nowhere else. The scissors on the table are for cutting the noodles; one cut is normal.
