How to eat Korean BBQ (Samgyeopsal)

What locals do without thinking, written down.

Korean BBQ (Samgyeopsal) arrives with a step that changes how it tastes, and most visitors miss it because nobody thinks to mention it. Get the order wrong and the dish is fine but flat.

How locals eat it

  1. Grill until golden — Staff often cut and turn it for you. Let it brown.
  2. Make a ssam — Lettuce or perilla leaf, meat on top, then garlic.
  3. Add ssamjang — A dab of soybean paste. Wrap it and eat in one bite.

Good to know

Don't flip the meat too often — once is usually enough. Let one side brown properly before turning. Rice is not included here; order it separately (공기밥, about ₩1,000–2,000). Side dishes are free and refillable. Most shops require a minimum of two portions, so this is a dish for two or more. At the end, ask for 볶음밥 (bokkeumbap) — they fry rice in the pan with the leftover fat and kimchi. It is the traditional way to close a Korean BBQ meal.
