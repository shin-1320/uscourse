About UsCourse

UsCourse helps travelers explore Korea without the guesswork — showing whether a place welcomes solo diners, accepts foreign cards, has menus in your language, how spicy the food really is, and how locals actually eat each dish. Data is curated from public sources and enriched by traveler reviews.
